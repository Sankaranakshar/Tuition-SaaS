The direction
Warm off-white ground, ink text, one deep pine-green accent. Near-monochrome: only the accent and a muted brick red carry colour. Aging and at-risk read as neutral grey plus a text label — no amber. Tight radii, borders over shadows, dense but quiet. The rail is wider with persistent labels; People → Students, Schedule → Classes (Money and Inbox keep their names).
